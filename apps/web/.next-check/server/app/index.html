    How it works

    From new lead to booked test drive
    before your coffee cools.

    Agents read the lead, the vehicle and the conversation history before they write a word. Your people step in when a human touch closes the deal.

    Your AI team

    Seven specialists per building.
    Zero sick days.

    Hire the agents you need. Each one knows your inventory, your offers and your voice, and anything risky waits for a human yes.

    V

    Internet closer

    Answers every new lead in under a minute by text and email, qualifies the buyer and books the test drive.

    N

    Follow-up

    Revives quiet and aged leads with up to three personal messages, then lets them go politely.

    L
