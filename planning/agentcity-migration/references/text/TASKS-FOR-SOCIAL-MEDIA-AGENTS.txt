TASKS FOR SOCIAL MEDIA AGENTS (FAST SALES AND DEALERSHIPS)

Content Strategy
Develop weekly and monthly social media strategies based on business goals, audience, products, and promotions.
Content Calendar
Create and maintain a 30-day posting calendar, with topics, platforms, dates, and scheduled publishing times.
Content Creation
Write captions, hooks, calls to action, hashtags, and platform-specific posts. Prepare creative briefs for images, carousels, and videos.
Graphic and Video Production
Generate branded graphics, promotional images, short-form videos, reels, and story assets using approved creative tools.
Multi-Platform Publishing
Schedule and publish approved content to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, and other connected accounts.
Community Engagement
Monitor comments, mentions, and direct messages. Answer approved FAQs and escalate sensitive inquiries to a human.
Lead Generation
Create campaigns encouraging users to visit the website, request information, register for courses, or contact a dealership.
Trend Monitoring
Identify relevant industry trends, popular topics, and content opportunities without blindly copying competitors.
Competitor Analysis
Review publicly available competitor content, posting frequency, messaging, and engagement patterns.
Performance Analytics
Track reach, impressions, engagement, clicks, leads, conversions, and follower growth.
Content Optimization
Identify successful posts, test new approaches, and recommend improvements based on actual results.
Weekly Reporting
Produce weekly performance summaries, completed-work logs, upcoming schedules, and recommendations.
